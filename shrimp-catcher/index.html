Shrimp Catcher

The Wise Old Man has some tips on how to grow as an amateur angler.

Shrimp Catcher is a secondary tutorial quest meant to teach you the basics of the Fishing skill.

Overview

The Wise Old Man has some tips on how to grow as an amateur angler.

Walkthrough

To start this quest, create a coarse net at the crafting table. This will trigger the quest for you to speak to the Wise Old Man in Bramblemead Valley. Speak to him and choose option 2. This will make him remark about how you made yourself a very useful tool (remarking about the net) and remember in his very distant youth about spending countless hours catching something in a local pond with a net and how the owners weren't happy. He then says about how you can find yourself a nice spot to relax and wile away the dull hours of daylight between adventures and to challenge yourself to finding that single pristine fish. He continues about how he hadn't eaten a good fish in ages and asks you to catch him a sardine in the Brynmoor waters, but tells you that you'd need to use a fishing rod to catch one and craft bait to aid you.

Note: This will start the next objective, which is to capture a raw sardine.

He then begins to tell you that you can boil, roast, stew, and make a lot of delicious meals out of them. He also says that if you find really nice ones, you can put them on display. After this, you will be presented with four options to discuss fishing.

Gone Fishin!

Item(s) required: Any fishing net, Ash Rod.

In order to get to the sardines, you will need to get some Fishing levels.

Head to any nearby net fishing spot and stand within it while wielding your net to start trawling to capture raw shrimp, and you'll unlock shrimp bait. Keep capturing shrimp using your net until you reach level 3, where you'll unlock the ability to create Fishing Rods. Go to your crafting table and use 1 ash log and 1 coarse thread to make an ash rod, then with the shrimp you've caught, create some shrimp bait (each shrimp gives 1 bait).

Once you have your fishing rod and bait, head to a nearby rod fishing spot and fish there.

Unlike with net fishing, you will need to equip both your fishing rod and bait to start rod fishing. Hold down your cast button (LMB by default) and release it to start fishing, but it is possible to miss the spot if you cast too weakly or too strongly. Once you've successfully cast into the fishing spot, you will need to use your strafe left/right buttons (A and D buttons respectively) to tire out the fish. A bar will appear above the bottom-middle of your screen that will gradually disappear. If the bar remains blue, you will begin to tire out the fish, but if it is red, that means the fish is stronger and reeling in the fish will consume greater amounts of stamina. When the fish is tired out, an option will appear to reel in the fish.

When the bar is empty, you will have caught a fish. This can either be a sardine or a trout.

Note: Fishing will consume your stamina rapidly. If you do not reel in your fish quickly before depleting your stamina, your fish will escape.

Once you have caught your sardine, head back to the Wise Old Man.

Back to the grind!

Bringing the sardine you've caught back to him, the Wise Old Man will remark how you've caught a wonderful delicious sardine and how it brings back old memories. Before cooking the sardine like how his grampa used to, he rewards you for a job well done.

Congratulations! Quest complete!

Rewards

  • 1x Tome of Fishing - Vol 1
  • 3x feather bait
