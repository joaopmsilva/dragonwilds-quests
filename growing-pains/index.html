Growing Pains

Learn the basics of the Farming skill from the Wise Old Man.

Growing Pains is a secondary quest that teaches the player how to use the Farming skill.

Overview

Description
Learn the basics of the Farming skill from the Wise Old Man.
Start Point
Create the wooden watering can or the wooden spade in the crafting table interface.
Type
Secondary Quest
Requirements
None
Required items
  • 18 ash logs to build an ash farming plot, wooden spade, and a wooden watering can
  • 2 coarse thread to build the Wooden Spade and Wooden Watering Can
  • 3 wheat seeds (acquired during quest)
Recommended items
  • Building an ash farming plot near a pond
Enemies to defeat
None

Walkthrough

Make and receive either the wooden watering can or the wooden spade in your inventory. This will trigger a quest to start talking to the Wise Old Man at Bramblemead Valley, where he will give you the basics of the Farming skill. Ensure that at least one inventory space is available to receive the wheat seeds.

Building a plot

After talking to the Wise Old Man, create an ash farming plot and dig the plot whilst wielding the wooden spade. Dig five times, and you will receive at least three weeds and 3 Farming experience. It is recommended to store the weeds inside a compost bin to create compost for future plots. After digging the wheat plot, place the wheat seeds in your inventory (or equip the seeds) and left-click on the plot. You will receive 5 Farming experience for planting the wheat seeds. After spreading the seeds, you will need to water the wheat plot at each stage by equipping a full watering can. You can fill your watering can by pressing the F key on a pond of water. Water the wheat plot five times until you see a check mark at the bottom of the watering icon. You will need to wait half an in-game day (12 minutes client-side) for the wheat chute to grow into a wheat stalk.

Once the wheat chute grows into a wheat stalk, water the plot five times again. At any time during the process, not watering the plant after six in-game hours after the next phase will result in the wheat chute becoming diseased. You will not be able to create plant cure, so you will have to start again.

After waiting another in-game day, the wheat stalk will turn into harvestable wheat. Harvest the wheat before talking to the Wise Old Man again, and he will reward you with a pack of Farming supplies.

Rewards

  • Spare farmer's supplies, containing:
    • Plant cure
    • Tome of Farming - Vol 2
  • Level 3 Farming
    • The ability to make plant cure

Trivia

  • There is a similar quest name in RuneScape and Old School RuneScape called A Fairy Tale I - Growing Pains.
