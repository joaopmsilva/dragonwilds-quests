Dog Days is a secondary quest given by Vannaka in the Stormtouched Highlands.

Overview

Walkthrough

Investigate the Garou and their rituals

Talk to Vannaka, who is standing under a cliff in the eastern part of the Highlands (see the image). He will tell you about the garou and ask you to investigate their rituals.

Go to the location of the lore book (at ritual of purification), beneath the wyrm corpse, and read it. It is advised to go around and approach from the north, as there are fewer enemies. There is a garou thane next to the book, but facing the opposite direction, so you can sneak there and read the book.

Challenging the King

After you read the book, you have to defeat the Garou King and get his tainted token. Go to the castle in the north-west of the Highlands. Use Windstep as much as you can, to avoid as many enemies as you can. At the top of the hill, in the castle, you need to defeat the king, who is a thane, and his attacks are the same. It is suggested to cast Windstep onto the arches of the Garou King's ruined temple to safespot its attacks.

Ritual of Purification

Go back to Vannaka after you get the token. He will tell you that the token is tainted and needs to be purified. Head north to the location of Lougrim's Shrine, kill the garou druids there, and interact with the shrine. Note that you will need 2 ram horns and 3 Shock Bulbs to complete the ritual, so kill rams and wild lapulis vines until you have enough materials. The ritual will then reward you with the purified token.

Go back to Vannaka and talk with him.

Congratulations, Quest complete!

Rewards

  • Garou highborn's cloak
