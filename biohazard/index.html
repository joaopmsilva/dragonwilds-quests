Biohazard is a secondary quest found in Dowdun Reach. To start, players must speak to Zanik.

Walkthrough

Getting started

You can start this quest in one of two ways: speak to Zanik, who is in a cell just after the hill exit of the The Lake, or simply collect a corrupted essence sample from any corrupted raven in Dowdun Reach. Either way, Zanik will ask you to gather 10 corrupted essence samples from corrupted ravens scattered across the region. Refer to the map on the right for their locations.

The three ingredients

Once you return to Zanik with all 10 samples, she will ask you to gather three additional items: Dragon blood, unholy water, and a twilight lily.

  • Dragon blood: can be obtained by slaying any dragon in Dowdun Reach, or from a dragon wolf if you want to head back to Fellhollow and hunt easier enemies.
  • Twilight lily: found in The Lake, glowing blue in front of an anima tree. The Lake is just south-west of Zanik.
  • Unholy water: behind The Cathedral, near three Zamorakian acolytes, a mage of Zamorak, and two elite Black Knights. Look for a statue with water flowing from its hands. You do not need to fight any of the enemies here — you can sneak to the fountain south-west of them to collect it.

Finding the Source

Bring all three items back to Zanik. She will ask you to track down the source of the corruption.

Head to The Nexus and make your way to The Grand Hall. Use windstep to climb up to the roof (beware of a Black Knight sniper and a Black Knight guardian), and you'll find a corrupted chicken named Putrilius.

Complete the dialogue with Putrilius.

Returning to Zanik

Return to Zanik and tell her what you found on the roof of the Grand Hall. She thanks you for your help with investigating the corruption and rewards you for your efforts.

Quest Completed!

Rewards

  • 1x Zamorak cape (and its recipe)
  • 50x infernal fragments
    • You can collect this by trading 10 corrupted essence samples with Putrilius.

Trivia

  • This quest is named after one of RuneScape's earliest quests, Biohazard.
    • Like the original quest, the player will gather samples and try to find a cure.
