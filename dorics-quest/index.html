Doric's Quest is a secondary quest from Doric within the region of Fellhollow. To start this quest, you must have partially completed Withering Heights and completed Seeking Salvation.

Overview

Description
Doric is looking for rare weapons in Fellhollow.

Speak to Doric

Walkthrough

Speak to Doric after completing Seeking Salvation, he will requests that you collect several rare weapons from stories he believe originated from Fellhollow!

Undead Ranger's Bow

He'll first ask for the undead ranger's bow, enter the Skekven Kara vault and locate a dragonkin effigy. Once you found the recipe, craft the bow and return to Doric.

If you already own the item, you do not need to craft it, the item will be kept.

Necromancer's Staff

Next he'll ask for the necromancer's staff, which can be found in a dragonkin effigy in Vekchenven Kara. Once you found the recipe, craft the staff and return to Doric.

If you already own the item, you do not need to craft it, the item will be kept.

Shadow Sword

The final search will be the shadow sword, which the recipe can be found as a rare drop from killing Zogres. They are located in Coalridge Pass, a cave within Emberwood, Eastern side of Silverthorn Keep and in Vekchenven Kara as the vault's boss. Once you find the recipe, craft it and return to Doric.

If you already own the item, you do not need to craft it, the item will be kept.

Congratulations! Quest complete!

Rewards

  • 1 Doric's smithing supplies.
  • 1 Doric's superior smithing supplies.
  • 1 The recipe to make the zombie axe.

Trivia

  • While the content of the quest is different compared to the RuneScape and Old School RuneScape versions, it does share the same name.
    • In RuneScape, the quest was reworked and renamed to What's Mine is Yours, while Old School RuneScape kept the same name
  • The Official news posts call the quest The Collector Calling, despite not using the name in-game.
