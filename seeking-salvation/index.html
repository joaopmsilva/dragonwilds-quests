Seeking Salvation

Seeking Salvation is a secondary quest where players help Doric search for more valuable relics.

Overview

Description
Find the statues to gain entrance to the vault and uncover the secrets that lie within.
Start Point
Speak to Doric east of the Lake of Lost Souls, inside a cave.
Type
Secondary Quest
Requirements
  • Partial completion of Withering Heights, to gain access to Fellhollow
Required items
  • 5 air runes to cast Windstep or resources to build foundations and stairs to certain statues
  • Iron pickaxe or better
  • Torch or the spell Fire Spirit (if too dark or in a cave)
Recommended items
  • Good armor and weapons
  • Food and water
Enemies to defeat
  • Zombies (Can appear outside of the vault)
  • Skeletons
  • Dragon wolves
  • Vault archon
  • Vault guardian hulk
  • Vault overlord
  • Spectral Chinchompas

Walkthrough

Starting off

The quest can be started by talking to Doric east of the Lake of Lost Souls, inside a cave ruin near the entrance to the Skekven Kara vault.

He'll inform you that the vault door is sealed by magic and requires you to unlock it. To unlock it, look for four dragonkin statues; activate them to unlock the door.

  1. One statue is located outside the cave entrance, before entering the cave.
  2. A statue can be found south-west of Doric, behind the giant pillar. If it is too dark, bring out your torch for better lighting.
  3. One is located on the top of the pillar, directly west of Doric. You will require the Windstep spell or build some foundation and/or stairs.
  4. The final statue is located north-west of Doric; there will be some rot. Use Windstep or build some stairs at the location. There will be a cracked wall; you will need a pickaxe to break it.

Skekven Kara

Once you have located and activated all four statues, you can enter the Skekven Kara vault. Make sure to bring plenty of cooked food and drinks (such as clean water or infusions) as there are multiple enemies within.

Inside the Skekven Kara vault, proceed in the entry room to the central staircase. At the second highest level of the stairs, an opening in the wall south of the staircase can be reached by using Windstep. In this cavern, interact with the dragonkin statue to learn the secrets, which Doric can translate.

Rewards

  • 1 salve crystal
    • This also unlocks the permanent option to purchase more crystals from Death's Exchange for 25 soul fragments each.
  • The recipe to craft a salve amulet

Required to Complete

This quest is required to complete the following quest(s):

  • Doric's Quest
