The Wild Hunt

Help Vannaka safeguard Fellhollow's nature spirits, by hunting them down in willow groves and collecting their hearts.

The Wild Hunt is a secondary quest from Vannaka within the region of Fellhollow. This quest introduces the player to a side story involving dying nature spirits due to the effects of Imaru's reign over Fellhollow and saving the remaining spirits with an unexpected yet welcomed twist.

Walkthrough

To begin this quest, speak to Vannaka who can be found west of Cathan's location in the Bleakfields Valley region of Fellhollow.

He will explain to you how nature spirits are dying out due to the nature of this region and he tasks you to find nature spirits and defeat them to collect their primordial hearts. These spirits can only be found in Willow groves at or around the western area of Fellhollow and will look like white glowing terrorbirds.

If you aren't up to looking all over for them, you can find one near the northwestern area of the druid's circle in the Emberwood area, one in the Dragon's Run area, and another in the southern section of Witchwillow Range.

Once you have all three hearts, return to Vannaka who will then use an old garou ritual from before Kuldra's reign to imbue you with the essence of these spirits.

Congratulations! Quest complete!

Rewards

  • Access to a terrorbird mount (press-hold the ALT key to use)
  • 50x soul fragments (for any extra hearts you have)

Required to Complete

This quest is required to complete the following quest(s):

  • Things That Go Boom In The Night
