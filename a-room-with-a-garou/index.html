A Room With A Garou

A Room With A Garou is a secondary quest in the Fractured Plains. It can be started by talking to Caratacus.

Overview

Description
An elder garou wants you to build a home that his late wife would be proud of.
Start Point
Speak to Caratacus in the northwestern corner of the Fractured Plains.
Type
Secondary Quest
Requirements
None
Required items
Ash logs or another building material to create a house around Caratacus.
Recommended items
At least 20 ash logs for basic building.
Enemies to defeat
None

Walkthrough

Caratacus laments the passing of his wife, Cliodhina, who wanted to live in the area. He'll ask you to help him build a home, as he's unable to do so due to his blindness.

You can build the house however you like, but to finish the quest quickly, building 4 walls and a roof within the floor space is recommended. This requires a minimum of 20 ash logs, where players can create:

  • 4 walls
  • 1 45° roof

Once completed, speak to him again.

Congratulations, Quest complete!
