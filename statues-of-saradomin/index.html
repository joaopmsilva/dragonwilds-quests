Statues of Saradomin

Released 19 May 2026

On this page

  1. Locations
  2. Walkthrough

Statues of Saradomin is an activity that can be started in the Bramblemead Valley, east of Crasorak Kara.

Locations

Walkthrough

The activity requires the player to find three statues of Saradomin and insert gemstones into them:

  1. Whispering Swamp: The statue is in the north-west corner of the swamp. The statue requires an opal to be inserted in exchange for the opal emblem
  2. Fractured Plains: The statue is in the central portion of the plains, east of the tower and west of a lake, on the lower side of a small cliff. The statue requires a jade to be inserted in exchange for the jade emblem
  3. Stormtouched Highlands: The statue is in the north-east portion of the highlands, along the cliff edge. The statue requires a sapphire to be inserted in exchange for the sapphire emblem.

Once all emblems are collected, return to the statue to receive a Saradominist cloak.
