The Nightmare Crucible is a combat activity found within the Umbral Sands region of Ashenfall. It is meant to act akin to RuneScape's Dominion Tower and Old School RuneScape's Nightmare Zone in where the player fights against all of the toughest enemies Ashenfall has to offer thus far.

To unlock a Nightmare Crucible, the player must use a corresponding item, called an Engram, whose title matches the Crucible of interest. An Engram only provides a single attempt to a given Crucible: - Single player: win or lose, the crucible will re-lock once the player enters it once. - Multiplayer: The crucible will only remain open as long as at least one player remains in it.

If the player dies while inside a Crucible, their gravestone will appear outside the crucible entrance.

The interior of Nightmare Crucibles are wide open, flat areas with almost no obstacles, save for the small Reward Pedestal in the center, and no safe spots for ranged or magic users. There is enough space to run around the arena in a circle and fight enemies from afar, but there is nowhere to hide. It is sometimes possible, depending on the player's positioning, to attract the attention of only a subset of the enemies in a given Crucible at a time.

Bug:
Within the Nightmare Crucible of Betrayal the Abyssal Demon is currently bugged and has about 64,300 HP.
Bug:
Within the Nightmare Crucible of Helplessness, if the player is against the outer wall, there is a possibility of the Green Dragon spawning outside of render distance. If this happens, the Nightmare Crucible will complete without the Green Dragon being slain provided the Green Dragon never renders.

Enemies to Defeat

Nightmare Crucible of Humiliation - in Dunes of Uzzer (Northeast) - Unlock with Crucible Engram of Humiliation

  • 1 x blue dragon
  • 1 x green dragon
  • 1 x red dragon

Nightmare Crucible of the Hounded - in Manafem Plains (Southwest) - Unlock with Crucible Engram of the Hounded

  • 2 x zogre

Nightmare Crucible of Betrayal - in The Burning Spire (Southeast) - Unlock with Crucible Engram of Betrayal

  • 1 x Black Knight Titan
  • 1 x green dragon or abyssal demon

Nightmare Crucible of Helplessness - in Alcarrid Oasis (Northwest) - Unlock with Crucible Engram of Helplessness

  • 2 x garou thane
  • 1 x green dragon

Rewards

Enemies in Nightmare Crucibles do not drop anything upon defeat.

Upon killing all monsters in the Nightmare Crucible, one power level 7 ascension core is rewarded by interacting with the reward pedestal. In multiplayer sessions, additional players do not receive additional ascension cores. In general, one engram corresponds to one ascension core, assuming the crucible is cleared.

Note: Currently there is a bug where if Buddy, summoned from the Pharaoh's Sceptre special attack, is alive, the challenge is not counted as completed. Player must wait for Buddy to despawn for the challenge to complete.


You can despawn Buddy by casting your special with the pharaoh's sceptre
