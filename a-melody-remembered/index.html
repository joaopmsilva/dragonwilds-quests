A Melody Remembered

A jewellery box in Ghornfell plays a haunting melody, but it cannot complete its song.

A Melody Remembered is a secondary quest in the Stormtouched Highlands. It can be started by interacting with a Jewellery Box located south of the Garou king's fortress.

Overview

Description
A jewellery box in Ghornfell plays a haunting melody, but it cannot complete its song.
Start Point
Interact with a Jewellery Box on a desk located in Stormtouched Highlands, south of the Garou king's fortress, Highlands Castle.
Type
Secondary Quest
Requirements
Dragon Slayer completed (to access Amulet of Magic)
Required items
  • Inspiring ring of Runecrafting (or 1 clay mould, 2 silver bars, 2 opals, and 3 vault shards to create the ring)
  • Amulet of Magic (or 1 clay mould, 2 gold bars, 2 silver bars, 4 sapphires, and 6 vault shards to create the amulet)
  • Jeweler's bench (or 12 oak planks, 8 ground sandstone, 2 gold bars, 2 silver bars, and 1 vault core to create both the ring and the amulet)
Recommended items
None
Enemies to defeat
Several Garou

Walkthrough

Upon finding the music box, located in a ruined building south of the Garou King's fortress (Highlands Castle), follow the road to it; there is a Jewellery Box on a desk. Upon interacting with it, you will be asked to give it the following items:

  • An inspiring ring of Runecrafting
  • It will then ask for an amulet of Magic (not to be confused with the inspiring amulet of Magic.)

Congratulations, Quest complete!

Reward

  • 1x Ravanna's ring
    • Includes the recipe to crafting more
    • Deletes Salve Amulet if worn
