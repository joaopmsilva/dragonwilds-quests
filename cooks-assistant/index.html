Cook's Assistant

Cook's Assistant is a secondary quest in the Fractured Plains. Players can start by talking to Slopfinger.

Overview

Description
Help Slopfinger in Ghornfell bake a memorable cake for Manktongue.
Start Point
Speak to Slopfinger in the southwestern corner of the Fractured Plains.
Type
Secondary Quest
Requirements
Farming skill unlocked and wheat gathered.
Required items
  • 4 eggs
  • 7 wheats (One is for the cow)
  • 1 bucket of milk (can be obtained by cow outside of the building)
Recommended items
Ingredients to create a lodestone.
Enemies to defeat
  • Chickens for eggs (Alternatively cutting down trees works)
  • Goblins for wheat seed from their goblin packs

Walkthrough

Collecting the ingredients

To start the quest, speak to Slopfinger, located in the south-west area of the Fractured Plains, near the border of the Stormtouched Highlands. Speaking to him, you learn that he is trying to make a cake, but his assistant, Manktongue, is ignoring him, so he asks you to assist him in showing that he can prove his worth as a chef. He'll ask you to collect 4 eggs, 7 wheats and 1 bucket of milk. The bucket of milk is the easiest ingredient; there is a cow just outside of the building within a pen. Interact with it and go through all the options, BUT do not milk it, as it will willingly give you milk. You need additional 1 wheats to feed it.

For the rest of the ingredients, it is advised that you build a lodestone at the quest area so you can head over to Bramblemead Valley or Temple Woods to collect some eggs from chickens. Alternatively, you can chop down trees and get a bird nest. Killing goblins is required to collect wheat seeds, or looking through chests.

Finishing up

Once you collect all the ingredients, return to Slopfinger, and he will have you bake the cake for him. After baking the Glorious Cake at the Goblin Stove nearby, speak to him again, and he will ask that you speak to Manktongue and show him the cake. Speak to Slopfinger one last time to complete the quest.

Congratulations, Quest complete!

Rewards

  • Recipe to bake the Victoria sponge cake

Trivia

  • This quest is named after one of the first few quests in RuneScape's history, Cook's Assistant.
    • Just like in RuneScape, Old School RuneScape and RuneScape Classic versions, you are asked to collect eggs, wheat, and a bucket of milk.
      • Unlike the three games, this quest requires more than 1 egg and wheat.
