Things That Go Boom In The Night

Gather Spectral Chinchompa skulls so that Vannaka can help the land heal.

Things That Go Boom In The Night is a secondary quest in Fellhollow. This quest is a partial continuation of the side storyline of preserving the region of Fellhollow due to Imaru's reign by introducing a new spectral enemy to be found within the region and how the environment is reacting to the adventurer's presence.

Walkthrough

To begin this quest, speak to Vannaka, who can be found west of Cathan's location in the Bleakfields Valley region of Fellhollow. Just like The Wild Hunt, he will explain how the spirits in the area continue to be in torment, and task you to hunt for ten spectral chinchompas and bring him ten of their skulls. You must attack and/or kill them as quickly as you can, or they will vanish. They are located across Fellhollow, within wooded areas and groves (like the nature spirits).

Best location to find them near Vannaka is;

  • South Emberwood's druid circle.
  • Witchwillow Range's willow tree grove.

There should be enough for you to kill and collect their skulls. But if you missed one or a few, they take up to 10 in-game days to spawn, so other locations are;

  • In another willow tree grove north of Hope's Fall.
  • West of Doric's camp, where the first statue is before entering the cave.
    • Another is further west, near the shore of the Lake of Lost Souls.
  • In the south-eastern wood of Silverthorn Keep
  • In another willow tree grove in Silverthorn Keep.
  • Most eastern part of Silverthorn Keep, where a purification pool is located when heading into the keep.

Once you have all ten skulls, he will thank you, believing with your help Fellhollow may know peace again.

Congratulations! Quest complete!
