X Marks The Spot is a one time in-game activity where you have to find dead bodies around the the region of Fellhollow to uncover hidden rewards. Players will find four skeletons wearing red hoods and can start this trail by interacting with any of them. In order to complete this trail, players will need to have read Scathe's, Malcifer's and Henning's diaries to uncover where the rewards are buried. Players will, in order to start this trail, will have to have completed Dragon Slayer and partially started Withering Heights to enter the region.

Rewards

In the buried treasure from Fellhollow you will receive the following:

  • PLAN: spectral book
  • Scratchy sackcloth

And some various resources.

Trivia

  • The activity is named after a quest in Old School RuneScape, also called X Marks the Spot.
