The Fight Cave is a dangerous combat activity located within The Burning Spire, in Umbral Sands. It is introduced during the quest, Icthlarin's Little Helper, and is required to complete the quest's version of the cave before being able to do the real one.

Location

Monsters

In this activity, players will be faced with KotHaar which each wave increasing in numbers and power level. Before each wave starts, you only have ten seconds to prepare your weapons and buffs. Every wave has between two to twenty different KotHaar enemies and may contain a mini-boss or two. Each wave will eventually have the arena itself shooting firebolts around the arena. The notable monsters are:

  • KotHaar-Hur
  • KotHaar-Ket
  • KotHaar-Xil
  • KotHaar-Kal
  • Tok-Xil

Waves

During the quest Icthlarin's Little Helper, the activity only has one option available: Trials of Fuzan. This acts as a tutorial for players with only ten waves to fight before fighting Fuzan. After completing the quest, the ability to fight in the full activity is unlocked. This will show up as "Start the Fight Caves" and make the player fight through thirty waves of KotHaar before fighting Fuzan. The final wave will have two Tok-Xil.
